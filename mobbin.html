Design system

Mobbin

An analysis of Mobbin's design language — a gallery-white, monochrome interface system built to disappear behind the content it curates. Near-black ink on white canvas, a ladder of barely-there neutral tints instead of shadows, stadium-pill controls, 24px card geometry, iOS-style 30% squircle icon tiles, and the Saans typeface at unusual variable weights (652 display, 456 text, 300 light). One electric blue accent is reserved for commercial signals; every other color on screen belongs to the product screenshots being showcased.

Source: getdesign.md/design-md/mobbin/DESIGN.md · rendered as an unofficial static reference for readability, not affiliated with Mobbin.
